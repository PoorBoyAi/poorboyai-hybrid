Ask PB. Answered on your device.

PB is PoorBoyAI's AI agent — he lives in our Telegram community, and he knows a whole lot more than crypto. Ask him anything: science, history, writing, everyday how-tos, or PoorBoyAI itself. Now he answers on the web too. In Chrome on desktop, Gemini Nano generates the answer right on your own machine. Everywhere else, the same question rides to cloud Gemini. The badge on every answer tells you which lane served it. And when a question needs today's facts — a price, the news, anything live — cloud PB searches the web first and lists the sources he checked under the answer.

How this works

On-device · Gemini Nano

Chrome 139+ on desktop ships Gemini Nano through the Prompt API. Firebase AI Logic's hybrid mode prefers it: your question never leaves your machine, answers cost nothing, and they work offline.

Cloud · Gemini

No Nano? No problem. The same SDK call falls back to a cloud-hosted Gemini model through the Gemini Developer API — same PB, same knowledge, same voice. The cloud lane also carries live Google Search grounding, so questions about today's world are answered from sources PB actually checked.

Honest badge

The badge isn't decoration. Firebase AI Logic reports inferenceSource on every response, and the badge shows exactly the lane the SDK says served your answer.
